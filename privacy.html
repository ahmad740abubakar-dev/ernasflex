Privacy Policy

Draft for review. This preview does not collect or store the waitlist form data. Publish a final version only after the production data flows and service providers are confirmed.

Information we may collect

When the production platform is built, it may process account details such as name, email, phone number, account security records, task submissions, referral attribution, wallet ledger entries, withdrawal requests, support messages, and relevant payment references. Bank details should be collected only when necessary and protected with restricted access.

Why information is used

Information may be used to operate accounts, deliver tasks, verify payments, prevent fraud, calculate rewards, process withdrawal requests, provide support, and meet legal obligations. The final policy must identify the lawful basis for each relevant processing purpose.

Payment information

Card and payment credentials should be handled by the authorised payment provider. EarnFlex should store only necessary payment references and verification results, never raw card details or secret API keys.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by contracted hosting, database, authentication, email, payment, and security providers as needed to run the service. The final policy should name the actual providers, describe relevant transfers, and explain applicable safeguards.

Retention and security

Data should be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purpose or legal requirements. The production build should use access controls, secure secret storage, server-side authorization, backups, and an incident response process. No website can promise absolute security.

Your choices and rights

The final policy should explain how users can request access, correction, deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy complaint, subject to lawful record-retention requirements.
