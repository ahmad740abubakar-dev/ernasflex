Refund Policy

Draft only — complete and approve this policy before taking any registration payments.

Registration fee

The planned registration fee is ₦2,500. Before launch, EarnFlex must specify exactly what membership provides and when the service is considered delivered. This draft does not establish a final no-refund or limited-refund rule.

Requesting a refund

Users should be able to contact support with their registered email, payment reference, date, and reason for the request. Never ask users to send passwords, full card numbers, or one-time security codes.

Duplicate or failed payments

Where a payment appears duplicated or a payment succeeds but access is not provided, EarnFlex should investigate against the payment provider's verified records and resolve the issue under applicable law and provider rules.

Decision and timing

Before launch, publish the support contact, review process, target response time, any applicable refund timing, and escalation route. Do not promise a refund until eligibility has been checked and the refund has actually been initiated.

Applicable rights

This policy does not remove rights users may have under applicable law or payment-provider rules. Final terms require review for the actual service and jurisdiction.

Do not accept live payments while this policy and the underlying business model remain unfinished.
