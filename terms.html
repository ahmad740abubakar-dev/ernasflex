PLATFORM POLICIES

Terms & Conditions

Draft for review — not yet approved for launch. Have this document reviewed for your business and applicable Nigerian law before accepting payments.

1. About EarnFlex

EarnFlex is intended to connect members with legitimate digital tasks and assignments. The platform is not a promise of employment, guaranteed tasks, or guaranteed income.

2. Eligibility and accounts

Users must provide accurate information, protect their login credentials, and use only accounts they are authorised to operate. Users must not create duplicate accounts, impersonate others, or bypass platform controls.

3. Registration fee and payment

The proposed registration fee is ₦2,500. Before launch, EarnFlex must clearly disclose what the fee pays for, when access begins, any applicable taxes or payment charges, and the refund process. A payment is recognised only after server-side verification with the payment provider. Do not pay based solely on a screenshot or browser message.

4. Tasks and submissions

Each task should show its instructions, reward, acceptance criteria, deadline, and required evidence before a user starts. Users must submit original, truthful work and follow applicable law and third-party platform rules. EarnFlex may reject incomplete, fraudulent, plagiarised, or non-compliant submissions with a reason and any resubmission option.

5. Rewards and wallet records

Rewards are credited only after task approval and are subject to the stated task budget. Pending rewards are not withdrawable. The wallet is a record of platform liabilities, not a bank account or deposit account. EarnFlex must maintain sufficient funds for rewards it authorises.

6. Referrals

The planned referral bonus is ₦200. It becomes eligible only when the referred user has paid a verified registration fee and completed their first approved task, subject to anti-fraud checks. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, misleading promotions, and referral manipulation are prohibited. Referral rewards are not guaranteed before eligibility is met.

7. Withdrawals

The planned minimum withdrawal request is ₦5,000 of available balance. Requests are reviewed by an administrator and may require identity and bank-account verification. The live policy must state processing targets, permitted reasons for review or rejection, dispute channels, and how users will be notified. EarnFlex must not represent a request as paid until the transfer is confirmed.

8. Refunds and complaints

Registration fee refunds will be governed by a separately published Refund Policy, applicable law, and payment-provider rules. Users should be given a clear contact channel and a fair way to dispute a payment, task decision, or withdrawal.

9. Suspensions and prohibited use

EarnFlex may investigate suspected fraud, abuse, unlawful activity, or security risks. Restrictions should be proportionate, documented, and subject to an accessible review or complaint process where appropriate.

10. Changes and contact

Material policy changes should be announced before they take effect where required. Replace the placeholder support details and publish a business address/contact before launch.

This draft is a starting point and is not legal advice. Do not publish it as final until the fee model, refunds, withdrawal service levels, data handling, and local compliance have been reviewed.
